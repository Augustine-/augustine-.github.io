Pay for it forever

How much would you need in Treasury bills for the interest alone to pay for a subscription?

  • To pay today's price, before tax
  • After federal tax
  • Forever, as the price keeps rising
The math

A year of the subscription costs twelve times the monthly price. Divide that by the yield and you have the balance whose interest pays for one year at today's price.

Tax takes a share of the interest, so after tax you divide by the yield times one minus your bracket instead.

If the price rises every year, the interest has to rise with it, so part of each year's interest must stay invested. The balance you need is the yearly cost divided by the after-tax yield minus the yearly price increase. When the price rises as fast as the interest or faster, that difference is zero or below, and no balance is ever enough.
